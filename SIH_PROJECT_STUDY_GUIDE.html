City-Wide AI Traffic Intelligence Engine

Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Complete Study Guide

1. The Problem Statement (Why we built this)

  • Urban Congestion: Traffic jams waste time and fuel. Cities are growing faster than road networks.
  • Disconnected CCTVs: Cities have thousands of cameras, but they don't "talk" to each other. Humans cannot watch 1,000s of screens manually.
  • Hard to Track Vehicles: Tracking a specific vehicle (e.g., a speeding car or a criminal) across the city grid manually is nearly impossible.
  • Lack of Real-Time Alerts: Traffic police need instant, automated alerts for over-speeding, sudden congestion, and unusual routes, rather than finding out after it happens.

2. How We Solved It (Our Features)

We built an AI-powered command center that connects all city cameras into one smart network. Instead of humans watching screens, AI does the heavy lifting.

A. Command Dashboard

  • What it is: A bird's-eye view of the city's traffic health.
  • Benefit: Live KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) show active alerts, total vehicles detected, and live camera statuses in one premium interface.

B. Cross-Camera Tracking (Vehicle Journey Replay)

  • What it is: Search for any License Plate, and the system reconstructs its exact path across multiple cameras on a map.
  • Benefit: Cinematic Google Maps interface with glowing trails and an auto-scrolling timeline makes forensic investigations instant and easy.

C. Automated Alert Engine

  • What it is: A real-time queue that detects violations instantly.
  • Benefit: Automatically flags vehicles for High Speed, Unusual Routes, and triggers Congestion warnings. Operators can click to resolve alerts in real-time.

D. Live Traffic Analytics

  • What it is: Real-time charts analyzing vehicle flow and speeds.
  • Benefit: Automatically categorizes road congestion into LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH based on dynamic average speed tracking.

3. Technology Stack (How we built it)

1. Computer Vision & AI (The Brains)
  • YOLOv8 (You Only Look Once): Used for high-speed Object Detection. It scans camera frames to find cars, trucks, and bikes instantly.
  • PaddleOCR (Optical Character Recognition): Used for ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition). It extracts text (license plates) from the bounding boxes detected by YOLO.
  • DeepSORT: Used for Vehicle Re-Identification (Tracking). It assigns a unique ID to a car so the system remembers it as it moves across different frames and cameras.
2. Frontend (The UI / Dashboard)
  • React.js 19 & Tailwind CSS v4: Used to build the dark, premium, enterprise-grade command center interface quickly and cleanly.
  • React-Leaflet & Google Maps: Powers the cinematic map trajectories and live camera plotting without needing expensive API keys.
3. Backend (The Engine)
  • Python 3.10+ & FastAPI: Handles the heavy lifting. We use Python's asyncio to simulate a real-time, tick-based environment for the SIH prototype presentation.
